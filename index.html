How EVENTO works

1. School registers once.
2. Each student signs up independently with their full name, class, roll number, Gmail and phone; EVENTO generates a unique Student ID.
3. School verifies the student's identity.
4. Verified students may choose a Sports event, a Cultural event, both or neither.
5. The school reviews and approves the event request.

For student safety, collect only necessary information and obtain school and parent/guardian permission before registering minors.
